I am a mathematician working in the field of Topological Data Analysis (TDA). During my PhD, at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, I developed new topological invariants for Morse-Smale vector fields, inspired by connections between persistent homology and Morse theory.
{: #clemens}

I recently moved to Trieste, where I joined the Laboratory of Data Engineering (LADE) and started the Master in Data Management and Curation (MDMC) at Area Science Park and SISSA.

Research interests:

- Topology of Morse-Smale vector fields
- Differentiability of zigzag persistence
- Interpretability of neural networks
