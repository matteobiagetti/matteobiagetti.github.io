I am starting a PhD in Applied Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Trieste, based at AREA Science Park. I obtained both my Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and my Master’s degree in Statistics at the University of Bologna. During my Master’s thesis, I worked on time series forecasting for air quality prediction.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of statistics, mathematics, and machine learning, with a particular interest in data-driven approaches to complex problems. More recently, I have become interested in topological data analysis and machine learning interpretability.
