I am completing my Master’s degree in Mathematics for Data Science at University of Trento and am enrolled in a pre-PhD program at Area Science Park. I obtained my Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics from University of Milano-Bicocca and have a background in statistics and stochastic processes.
I am particularly interested in Topological Data Analysis. In my Master’s thesis, I investigated the integration of multiparameter persistence into neural networks to improve their explainability.

Research interests:

- TDA for explainable models by design
- Interpretation of LLMs via topological methods
- Interplay between TDA and stochastic dynamics
